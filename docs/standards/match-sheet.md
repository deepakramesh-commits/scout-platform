# Match sheet standard

The club coordinator records who played, where, and for how long. It takes about ten minutes on a phone and works
without a connection: entries are kept on the phone and sent when the connection returns.

## Before kick-off

- For each team: the eleven starters and the substitutes, picked from the squad list, each with a shirt number
  and a position (GK, CB, FB, DM, CM, AM, LW, RW, ST).
- Exactly one starting goalkeeper. Shirt numbers are unique within a team.
- A player missing from the squad list is added by the club before the match, never typed in by hand.

## During and after the match

- Substitutions: minute, player off, player on. At most five (six with extra time).
- Goals: minute and scorer; own goals are recorded against the player who scored them.
- Cards: minute and player. A second yellow sends the player off.
- The sheet checks every entry as it is typed: a substitute must be on the bench, the player going off must be on the
  pitch, a scorer must be on the pitch at that minute.

## Why it matters

Minutes played come from the sheet, and per-90 numbers divide by them. The quality check flags any event by a
player who is not on the sheet, by a player already substituted, or by a substitute before he came on.
