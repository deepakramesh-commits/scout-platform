# Verification standard

A profile is marked **verified** only when a field officer or the federation has checked all of these. Until
then every screen marks it unverified.

## What is checked

1. **Federation registration.** The player's registration number with the national federation or league,
   checked against the federation's own records.
2. **Identity.** A government ID (passport or national ID card) seen in person. The legal name and date of birth
   are recorded; the document image is stored privately and shown only to admins.
3. **Photo.** A current photo taken by the field officer, matched to the ID.
4. **Contract status.** Under contract, free agent or amateur, from the club or the federation.

## Who sees what

Scouts see only that a profile is verified and the player's birth year. The legal name, the full date of birth
and the ID document never reach the scouting side.

## Age

An age is documented when the ID and the federation registration agree on the date of birth. A player under 18
appears to clubs only after a parent or guardian has consented and one of our admins has approved the profile.
