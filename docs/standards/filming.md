# Filming standard

Every match is filmed the same way, so every analyst sees the same picture and every number means the same thing.

## Equipment

- One fixed camera that sees the whole pitch: an independent auto-follow camera where the venue has one, a phone
  rig on a tripod where it does not. Never a competitor's camera system.
- A power bank or mains power for the full match, and a spare memory card.

## Camera position

- Raised, on the halfway line, as high as the venue allows (a stand, a roof, a scaffold of at least 4 m).
- Wide angle: both penalty areas in frame when the ball is in midfield.
- Do not zoom during play. If the camera follows the ball, keep the ball in the middle third of the frame.

## Recording

- Start before kick-off and stop after the final whistle. Keep recording through half time, or start a new file
  for the second half and upload both.
- 1080p or 720p, 25 or 30 frames a second, MP4 (H.264) or MOV. No filters, no music, no editing.
- Name the file `<home>-<away>-<yyyy-mm-dd>`.

## Upload

- Upload on the match's page within 24 hours. The upload resumes after a dropped connection, so start it on
  venue wifi and let it finish later if it must.
- The club coordinator fills the match sheet before or right after the match (see the match sheet standard).
